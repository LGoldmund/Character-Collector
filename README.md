Ever thought while watching any show: "who is this again?"? 
This small macOS desktop app answers this question!

The idea is to create lists of characters of series that you are watching to keep track of characters, clans, groups, familie trees etc!
You can add series and then add characters, by adding their name, their class/group and the title they have within that class/group.
Then you can add either a downloaded image or take a screenshot directly that will be stored inside the app dict.

Suggestions are welcome!
Also please report any bugs.
